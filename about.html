About Our Ministry

Guided by Divine Grace, Committed to Community Transformation

Christ's Love Ministries in Mysuru stands as a beacon of faith, dedicated to spreading the message of love, hope, and restoration. Founded by Ruben Ranjith, our ministry encompasses spiritual guidance, community uplifting, youth education, and charitable care.

Our Vision

Rooted in gospel teachings and daily spiritual devotion, we envision touching lives with divine compassion and building a vibrant community fellowship centered on faith, hope, and love across Mysuru and beyond.

Our Mission

Empowering youth and children with educational assistance, supporting families with prayer intercession, and serving the community through worship gatherings, daily devotionals, and selfless outreach.
