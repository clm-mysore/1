About Our Ministry

Guided by Divine Grace, Committed to Community Transformation

Christ's Love Ministries in Mysuru stands as a beacon of faith, dedicated to spreading the message of love, hope, and restoration. Founded by Ruben Ranjith, our mission encompasses spiritual guidance, community uplifting, youth education, and charitable care.

Through our worship services, digital devotions, and localized prayer groups, we strive to touch lives with divine compassion and build a vibrant fellowship rooted in love.
