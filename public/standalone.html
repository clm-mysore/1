About Our Ministry

Guided by Divine Grace, Dedicated to Community Transformation

Christ's Love Ministries Charitable Trust (R) in Mysuru stands as a registered beacon of faith, dedicated to spreading the message of love, hope, and restoration. Established by Founder Ruben Ranjith & Nandini Ruben, our ministry encompasses spiritual guidance, community uplifting, youth education, career guidance, and pastoral care across Karnataka.

Our Vision

Rooted in gospel teachings and daily spiritual devotion, we envision touching lives with divine compassion and building a vibrant community fellowship centered on faith, hope, and love across Mysuru and beyond.

Rooted in Christ's Unconditional Love

Our Mission

Empowering youth with spoken English and career mentoring, supporting families with prayer intercession, and serving the community through Sunday worship gatherings, free fellowship meals, and dedicated spiritual guidance.

Serving Every Family with Humility
